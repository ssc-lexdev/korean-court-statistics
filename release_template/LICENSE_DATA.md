## Source attribution
The values are digitized from the *Judicial Yearbook* (사법연감), 2002–2025 editions, published by the National Court Administration of the Supreme Court of Korea (법원행정처) on the Judicial Statistics Portal. Credit the source as:

> National Court Administration, Supreme Court of Korea. *Judicial Yearbook* (사법연감), 2002–2025 editions. Judicial Statistics Portal.

## What is not included
The original Yearbook PDF and Excel files are **not redistributed** in this package. `data/source_manifest.csv` lists each file with its SHA-256 checksum and retrieval route, so users can obtain and verify the originals from the portal.
