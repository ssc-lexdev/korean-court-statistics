# Known issues and limitations

Issues that users should know before analysis. None of them is an unresolved extraction error in a released value: every released value with a validation warning was re-read and resolved (see `validation/validation_protocol.md` §4). Report new issues to the authors; confirmed issues will be added here and fixed in a new version.

## Definitions and structural breaks (`data/structural_breaks.csv`, `break_flag`)
1. **Mean disposition time changes method in 2007.** For 2002–2006 the printed mean is an approximation from bin representative values; from 2007 it is the mean of case-level days ÷ 30 (`mean_months_bin_midpoint_2002_2006`). To compare across 2006/2007, recompute both years from the bins.
2. **The civil single-judge duration row includes small claims from 2010** (`civil_single_incl_small_claims_2010`).
3. **Family overall table:** there is no 2016 level shift (an earlier draft flagged one).
   - The 2016 and 2018 editions' year-by-year table prints 2016 values that double-count the appeal row (+3,017 filings, +2,930 dispositions). This is an official source-table error, corrected in later editions; the released values follow the corrected editions (`correction_log.csv`, `official_source_table_error`).
   - Non-litigation filings peak in 2008 (`family_noncontentious_2008`, suspected) and grow after 2013 partly through new statutory case types (`family_noncontentious_categories_2013_2018`). Both are registry entries without row flags (`row_flagged = False`).
4. **Stock–flow gaps:** the gap for civil main and family litigation is −2.7% / −3.3% in 2007 (`stock_flow_gap_2007`; cause suspected, not verified). The small medical-custody category misses the identity by more than 2% in 5 of its 7 OCR years (2008, 2009, 2011–2013; by 5 to 22 cases), with no identified cause. Receipts for 2004–2006 are current-year filings (`overview_receipts_current_year_2004_2006`).
5. **National pending 2002–2003** is spliced from a multi-year table (`pending_spliced_2002_2003`, `derived = True`).
6. **The court panel is unbalanced:** courts were established, converted and abolished during the period. Use `partial_year` and `coverage.csv`. Namyangju Branch Court small claims for 2018–2021 restate the Namyangju Si Court row (`dup_flag`).

## Source-documentation gaps
7. **The duration definition is quoted from the 2016, 2018 and 2020–2025 editions.** The general notes of earlier editions are not in the chapter files used, and the criminal section of the notes has no explicit duration rule. Reading appellate durations as starting from that instance's filing date is an inference.

## Provenance precision
8. **Some values have a page range rather than an exact page.** These are 89 detail and 263 duration values (`exact_page = False`), read in multi-page model requests and not located uniquely in the text layer. All other PDF values have an exact page.
9. **Six 2016-edition detail-table blocks have no printed-title evidence in `source_tables.csv`.** That edition's text layer is scrambled and the detail prompts did not request the title. The tables were located by page classification. The positional check (`validation/detail_position_check.csv`, 2026-11-20) rebuilt the tables from the text-layer word coordinates: it finds each block's printed title and confirms the row and column of all 432 values. Earlier checks: all 432 released values from these blocks occur in the PDF text layer of the pages sent, and in every block the 2007–2011 values (240 cells) agree with the 2011 edition, whose corresponding tables have title evidence (2026-11-20 check). The 2016-year values of these blocks are not released (the 2025 Excel edition is used); they differ from it in 6 cells: 4 are the family double count (item on the official table error) and 2 are civil by-instance dispositions (first instance 4,645,399 vs 4,645,402; total 4,746,992 vs 4,746,995), which the 2016 text layer prints as read, so they are source differences.
   A person checked three 2016-edition pages by copying text from the PDF viewer (`validation/text_layer_handcheck.csv`). All three agree with the automatic label `ok` because the characters are valid. But the reading order is scrambled: tables are emitted column by column, sentences are split across lines, and one printed title is reduced to its number. The automatic page label (`source_manifest.csv`) therefore means "valid characters", not "usable reading order". No released value depends on this: every 2016-edition value was read from page images of `02_2016년_사건의추이.pdf` (`source_type = ocr`). The other automatic labels were not hand-checked.
10. **Text layers of the 2015 (scanned/broken), 2017 and 2019 (broken encoding) PDFs** cannot be used for text-layer checks. Values from 2019 come from Excel. The 2015 and 2017 values were double-weighted in the re-reading sample.

## Extraction and validation
11. **Excel duration values (2019–2025 editions)** were structured by a language model from the Excel cell grid (`excel_llm_structured`). Every value was found in its source cell block, overlapping Excel editions agree on every shared value, and a rule-based re-read of the same blocks confirms the row and column of all 1,925 values (`validation/duration_excel_position_check.csv`).
12. **One human reader:** there was a single human reader (blind to extracted values, not to page context). The identifier of the independent Claude model used for re-reading on 2026-11-14 was not recorded.
13. **OCR values without a positional check:** the 249 overview values (single-year overview tables of the 2004–2018 volumes) were not compared with the text layer by position; the 6 of them from the 2015 and 2017 volumes, whose text layers are mis-encoded, were re-read from the page images by one author (`validation/overview_no_path_reread.csv`, all agree). All other OCR values were (`validation/validation_protocol.md` §7a); the check found three OCR errors, now corrected.
14. **Model reproducibility:** model identifiers are API aliases, with no snapshot or `system_fingerprint` recorded. The release is reconstructible from `model_responses/` but not guaranteed to be re-runnable (`docs/model_extraction.md`).

## Jurisdiction changes and court lineage (added after advisor Round 5)
15. **The civil first-instance split is not definition-invariant.** Monetary thresholds moved cases between the collegiate, single-judge and small-claims tables on 2015-02-13, 2017-01-01 and 2022-03-01 (statute text checked; `structural_breaks.csv`, `break_flag`). The years 2015 and 2022 are transition years. Use the derived `civil_main_1st_total` for a split-free series. The duration tables for civil collegiate and single-judge cases carry the same flags.
16. **Criminal collegiate filings have two unexplained level shifts:** 2012 (a one-year spike, identical in four editions) and 2024–2025. No change to Court Organization Act Art. 32(1) took effect in 2023–2025. Both are registered as `observed_only`; use `criminal_trial_1st_total`.
17. **Parent-court series fall when a branch, high court or bankruptcy court is created** (`court_events.csv`). Use district-wide sums (`parent_court_id_h`), or the event-specific aggregate in `recommended_user_action`. The 2014 territorial transfer to the Seoul Northern District Court is recorded, but its size was not quantified.
18. **Printed zeros before establishment.** The 10-year tables print current courts with 0 for earlier years (`pre_establishment = True`, `coverage_status = present_before_establishment`). Three units carry non-zero values before establishment that restate a predecessor's caseload: Namyangju small claims 2018–2021, Masan small claims 2007–2010, and Goyang small claims 2002. The Goyang value (5,790) equals the Goyang Si Court row of the 2002 edition.
